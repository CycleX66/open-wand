Train Spells

1. Calibrate (once)

Point the wand's tip straight up at the ceiling, hold it completely still, then tap Calibrate. This tells Open Wand which way your wand's sensor faces.

2. Teach it your spells

Pick a spell and cast it 3 or more times. Open Wand learns your version of each movement. Trained spells are recognised by Open Wand. Untrained ones still use the wand's own guess.

Smart Home

Spell actions

Spells can switch lights and other devices through Home Assistant, or call a web address (webhook). Addresses must start with https://, because the iPad blocks plain http from this page. For Home Assistant, use its remote https address and allow this page in its cors_allowed_origins.

How to Play

Casting

1. Wake the wand by giving it a flick. Connect it under Settings → Wand connection.
2. Hold the wand like a pen, squeeze the touch pads on the handle, and draw the spell shape in the air from the dot to the arrow.
3. Release the pads. The wand itself recognises the spell, lights up, and Open Wand records the cast.

The shapes shown here are guides. The wand's own built-in spell shapes are what count, so experiment if a spell won't trigger.

No wand?

Use "Cast without wand" on any spell to see the effect and trigger its smart-home actions.
